# Systematization & Automation of Media Reporting Across Ukraine  
### Business Analysis • Process Optimization • Data Automation • Change Management

## Overview
This project showcases the full cycle of business analysis and process automation for the nationwide media‑reporting system used by regional offices across Ukraine.  
Before the project, data on media publications, social media activity, and distribution of printed materials were collected manually, inconsistently, and in different formats (Excel, Word, email text). This resulted in:

- non‑standardized naming conventions (communication channels, topics, media types);
- month‑long delays in reporting;
- repeated back‑and‑forth file exchanges;
- dependency on a single specialist who manually cleaned and aggregated all data;
- high error rate due to strict punctuation requirements in the IT‑department’s automated counting script.

The goal of the project was to **analyze the existing process, eliminate bottlenecks, design a unified reporting system, and automate data collection and calculation**.

---

## Objectives
- Standardize data collection across all regions.
- Reduce reporting time from **1 months → sofort*.
- Eliminate manual aggregation and repeated file exchanges.
- Introduce unified naming conventions for:
  - communication channels,
  - topics,
  - media types,
  - printed materials.
- Expand the reporting scope:
  - publications in traditional media,
  - social media activity,
  - distribution of printed materials.
- Provide real‑time access to regional stock levels of printed materials.
- Train personnel and ensure adoption through documentation and internal regulations.

---

## Solution Architecture

### **1. Business Analysis & Process Mapping**
- Conducted interviews with regional coordinators.
- Mapped the AS‑IS process (data collection → manual cleaning → aggregation → reporting).
- Identified bottlenecks:
  - inconsistent formats,
  - lack of unified taxonomy,
  - dependency on one specialist,
  - high error rate due to punctuation rules.

### **2. Designing the TO‑BE Process**
- Unified data structure and naming conventions.
- Developed a single online form in **Google Sheets** with:
  - dropdown lists,
  - data validation,
  - automated calculations,
  - protected fields.

### **3. Automation**
- Integrated formulas for automatic counting.
- Eliminated punctuation‑dependent manual scripts.
- Enabled real‑time dashboards for:
  - media publications,
  - social media activity,
  - printed materials distribution.

### **4. Documentation & Change Management**
- Developed:
  - step‑by‑step instructions,
  - methodological guidelines,
  - internal regulation for mandatory use of the new system.
- Conducted training sessions for regional staff.

## Results

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Reporting time | 30–60 days | Instant (real‑time) |
| Data formats | Excel, Word, email text | Unified online form |
| Errors due to punctuation | High | Eliminated |
| Number of people involved | 25+ | 1 (automated aggregation) |
| Scope of reporting | Only media publications | Media + social media + printed materials |
