# TO-BE Process: Automated Media Reporting System

## Overview
Following an analysis of the process, a unified, automated system for collecting media reports was developed to ensure speed, accuracy and transparency.

Key improvements:
- A single online form in Google Sheets.
- Standardised categories:
  - communication channels,
  - themes,
  - types of media,
  - types of printed products.
- Automatic calculations:
  - Google Sheets formulas,
  - protected fields,
  - Elimination of human error.
- Reporting expansion:
  - publications in the media,
  - activity on social media,
  - distribution of printed products,
  - booklet stock in regions.
- Documentation and training:
  - instructions,
  - Methodological recommendations,
  - internal regulations.


## Benefits
- Reports are generated instantly.
- Data is received in a standardised format.
- No manual aggregation.
- Transparency and quality control.
- Reduced workload for regional coordinators.
